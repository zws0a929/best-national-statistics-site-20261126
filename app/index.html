About these numbers

Every figure on this site names its source and year. Headline figures come from the Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM) and the World Bank's World Development Indicators. Figures are shown as published; where two reputable sources differ (for example life expectancy), both are shown. This is an independent reference site, not affiliated with DOSM or the World Bank, and it works fully offline — no network requests, no tracking.
